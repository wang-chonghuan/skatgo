# SKATGO-212 rework

Two rounds after the frozen handoff, each its own commit.

1. **「铂金不好看，和银色也重叠了」** (f076d58). Platinum's shield was the picture's pale grey, and its star a hole onto
   the picture's teal ground — on the page a grey shield with a white cut-out, next to the grey silver medal. Now the
   shield is in the platinum tier's own teal (its picture's ground, the colour of its League card), lightness kept, and
   the star is filled white. Charter `ui.md` updated. Rechecked: AC1 (transparent corners, both boards, EN/DE,
   1280/375) and AC4 — passed; the League board looked at once.
2. **「奖杯能换颜色吗，比如橙色带光的效果的？但是还是同样风格，或者你认为其他颜色，或者是红色橙色火焰配色，但是还是奖杯」**, then
   after three colourings were shown on the board (A flame, B orange with a glow, C Legend's purple): **「b，稍微大点」**
   (10f5ed6). Legend's cup is orange (hue 28) with a light shine down its bowl and a soft orange glow behind it; the cup
   sits at 90% of the badge (80% in the version shown) so the glow has room. Charter `ui.md` and `ac.md` criterion 3
   updated (orange with a glow, in place of the gold cup). Rechecked: AC1, AC3 (orange, no green, the cup's shape, glow
   beside the stem) and AC4 — passed.

Net effect against the handoff: Bronze, Silver and Gold stay one medal in three metals and Diamond stays a shade darker;
Platinum is a teal shield with a white star instead of a pale grey one; Legend is an orange glowing cup instead of the
gold one. The independent look's Platinum note (faint, close to Silver) is settled by round 1.
