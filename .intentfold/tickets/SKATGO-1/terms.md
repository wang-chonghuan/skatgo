# SKATGO-1 terminology — decisions and basis

The research behind these decisions, with every source URL per concept, is in
`terms-research.md` (same directory, researched 2026-09-21). Key sources: the Internationale
Skatordnung (DSkV, 2022) for German, its authorised English translation (ISPA Canada, 2023), pagat.com
and English/German Wikipedia for everyday English/German usage, Chinese Wikipedia「斯卡特」and the
Chinese articles on 升级 and 桥牌 for Chinese.

Rule values the course states were all confirmed against ISkO 2022: Null 23/35/46/59, ladder
18·20·22·23·24·27·30…, lost game −2×, overbid scored at the smallest covering multiple and doubled,
tournament +50/−50 and 40 (3-player table) to each defender.

| suits | Kreuz, Pik, Herz, Karo | ISkO-DE, DE-WP |

| card points / trick | Augen / Stich | ISkO-DE |

## English — everyday English Skat (pagat.com, EN Wikipedia), official ISPA wording where it is also common

| Concept | Term used | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| declarer / defenders / partner | declarer / defenders / partner | Pagat, ISkO-EN |
| seats | forehand / middlehand / rearhand | ISkO-EN, Pagat |
| suits / ranks | Clubs, Spades, Hearts, Diamonds; Ace, King, Queen, Jack; J Q K A | Pagat, EN-WP |
| card points / trick / blanks | card points / trick / blanks (7-8-9 only) | EN-WP; "singleton" used for a lone 10, because "blank" also means singleton/void in EN-Gloss |
| bidding | bid, "Yes" / "Pass", overbid, passed in | ISkO-EN, Pagat |
| skat handling | pick up the skat, discard (put away), Hand | Pagat, EN-WP |
| value | base value, matadors "with 2 / without 2", "with 2, game 3", multiplier | Pagat, EN-WP; ISkO-EN says "Tops", less common in use |
| play | follow suit, trump, throw off, smear, draw trumps | Pagat, EN-Gloss. "Cash an ace" has no Skat source → the hints say "play/take your aces early" |

## Chinese — decided per concept

Chinese has no established Skat vocabulary: the only substantial Chinese source is the crowd-edited
zh-Wikipedia article, which itself mis-glosses several German terms. Where it differs from the
course, the course's term is kept when it is the established word of the Chinese card games the
learner already knows (升级, 桥牌, 斗地主) and changed only where the course's word means something
else in Chinese card vocabulary.

| Concept | Course had | zh-WP | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| putting 2 cards into the skat | 弃牌 / 弃掉 | 放置于一边 | **扣牌 / 扣下** (changed) | 弃牌 is "fold" in Chinese poker; 扣底 / 扣牌 is the 升级 word for burying cards face down |
| suit made void by discarding | 短门 | — | **断门** (changed) | 断门 is the attested 升级 word for a void |
| seats | 前家 / 中家 / 后家 | 先手 / 中间手 / 后手 | keep | 先手/后手 already mean "first/second move" in board games; 家 follows 上家/下家 of Chinese card games |
| card points | 点 / 牌点 | 牌分 | keep | 点 keeps card points apart from score 分, which the course needs in every settlement |
| base value / "game" | 基值 / 成局 | 基本分 / Spiel | keep | internal to the course's formula; zh-WP leaves Spiel untranslated |
| with / without | 有 / 无 | 带 / 缺 | keep | mirrors the German mit/ohne one-to-one and is how the course's formula reads aloud |
| bidding / answers | 叫牌 / 有・过 | 叫分 / 同意・放弃 | keep | 过 is the standard pass of Chinese card games; 叫分 is kept for the bid value (叫分阶梯) |
| defenders | 防守方 | 闲家 | keep | also used in the 升级 article; 闲家 reads as "idle player" |
| declarer, trick, follow, trump, ruff, throw off, trumps, side suits, draw trumps | 庄家, 墩, 跟牌, 主牌, 毙, 垫, 副牌, 清主 | same | keep | supported |
| lead, overbid, blanks, smear | 首出, 超叫, 白板, 送分 | — | keep | no Chinese source; 超叫 is also a bridge word (overcall) — noted, no better attested term |
