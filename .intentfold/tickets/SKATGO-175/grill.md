# SKATGO-175 grill

No question passes the bar: sizes are judged by looking, so they are built and shown.

## Decided for you
1. **How small?** 36px on PC, 28px on a phone — the colour cards' titles' size at each (the slogan was a quarter above them). First built at 26px on a phone; the look through the human's eyes found it then read smaller than the cards' titles.
   Basis: 「有点大」 asks for a step down, not a redesign; SKATGO-120 already took it from 66 to 48.
2. **How much space above?** 16px instead of 32px, PC and phone. Basis: 「top margin偏大」; the space under the title stays.
