## Q1 — how much of each icon the mark fills

- Header/rail mark `logo-96.png`: 88% (pad 6%) — shown at 40px with rounded corners.
- Installed icons 192/512 (rounded square): 80% (pad 10%).
- iOS home screen 180 (iOS rounds the corners itself): 76% (pad 12%), clear of the rounding.
- Maskable 512: the whole mark inside the 80%-diameter safe circle — pad 21%.

Reason: the tighter the icon is shown, the less room it can spare; the rounded and masked forms need
their corners clear.
