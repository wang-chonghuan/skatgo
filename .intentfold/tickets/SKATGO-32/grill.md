# SKATGO-32 grill

## Q1 — where hints go

The human delegated the placement (「你想想放哪，要有专门地方，还要能关闭，考虑也放消息区？」).

**Decision:** a message area directly under the info board, holding hints and refusals, each closable.

**Reason:** it is the one place neither the hand nor the drawer can cover, it is next to the other
information the learner reads, and it keeps the running line at the bottom for whose-turn text.
