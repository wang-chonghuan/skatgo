# SKATGO-141 grill

The human: 「叫牌时的文案删除太多，你给我恢复点……然后等检查」 — restore some, then they check. No question passes the bar;
decided, for their check:

1. **Which lines come back?** The three that say whom you bid with and what your answer means: the speaker's line, the
   asked line, the hold button — SKATGO-134's originals. Reason: without them a real table no longer says who is
   bidding, and "Yes" alone does not say it means playing that number too.
2. **Forehand alone?** Its short line ("Both passed – play at 18?") stays: it already says what happens.
3. **New wording or the originals?** The originals, as the course still shows them: they are known, translated and
   tested; 「恢复」 means bring back.
