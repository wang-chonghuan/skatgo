## What changed

**The pictures of both decks** (`app/brand/cards/`, new)

- 33 images generated with Azure OpenAI `gpt-image-2`, all high quality:
  - French courts K / D / B × 4;
  - German courts König / Ober / Unter × 4;
  - German Daus × 4 and German suit symbols × 4;
  - one back.
- **Cost:** 46 of the approved 70 calls. 5 of them were Azure server errors, retried.
- **How they came about:**
  - The six Herz style samples went through three rounds with the human: less empty space, then the Unter and Dame, then every prompt rewritten from scratch with a balanced palette. The human approved the result.
  - The other 27 were generated with the approved samples as style references.
  - The human confirmed the overview of both decks (ticket comment with the picture).
- **What is kept:**
  - `generate.sh` (the command), `prompts/` (style, format per kind, subject per image) and `calls.txt` (every call);
  - `masters/`: lossless WebP, pixel for pixel the model's PNGs (checked for all 33), each with its request metadata (no credential, no endpoint);
  - `PROVENANCE.md`: tool, model, dates, process, and that no commercial deck was copied or traced.
  - The 69 MB of PNGs stay out of git, in the main checkout's ignored `.intentfold/tmp/card-masters-png/` (the human's choice).
- **Served files:** `app/scripts/make-card-images.mjs` cuts them into `app/public/cards/` (33 WebP, 1.3 MB in all, about 39 KB per court; needs `cwebp` / `dwebp`).
  - Each picture is trimmed to its drawing.
  - Court tops are cut to half the court frame's shape, fitted by height (head to waist).
  - The German files ship but are not requested until SKATGO-67.

**The French deck in the product**

- `PlayingCard` (`playing-card.tsx`) draws its own face as one SVG (500 × 700 units):
  - **corner index** top left and turned bottom right: the rank as Bebas Neue outlines over the suit pip. Outlines, never text; `card-glyphs.ts` is generated by `app/scripts/extract-card-glyphs.py`.
  - **German pages** show B / D / K / A, **English pages** J / Q / K / A (from the page's locale).
  - **7–10:** the pips in their traditional places, the lower ones turned. **The ace:** one large pip.
  - **Courts:** the figure's top and its turned copy, overlapping one unit so no seam shows, in a frame notched around the two indices so the picture stays large.
  - **Pips:** ♣ ♠ ♥ ♦ drawn as paths, in the suit's colour.
  - The card keeps its sizes and states, and adds `data-deck="french"` and `data-index`.
  - The back is the generated ornament in the card's white frame.
- **Approved removals** (grill Q5; product.md was not touched):
  - (a) tokens: `fourColours`, `twoColours`, `fill.cardBack`, `color.cardBack`, `color.cardBackLight`;
  - (b) the dependency `@letele/playing-cards`, with its type shim;
  - (c) charter: `ui.md` and `engineering.md` describe the new deck, its pictures and how they are made.
- **The settings are gone** (grill Q6): the header's gear, the table's settings tab, the dialog, the store `lib/skat/settings.ts`, and the `settings_*` / `scheme_*` messages. SKATGO-67 brings back a choice of deck.

## Deviations

- **Grill Q2 changed on the samples.** The human asked for colours not dominated by red. Costumes now use a balanced palette, and the suit shows through its emblem (`grill.md`).
- **The proposed solution's "WebP, loaded per deck on demand"** is met per card: the browser fetches only the courts actually shown.
- **Not vectorised yet:** the German suit symbols (grill Q7, decided in SKATGO-67).
- **The court frame is notched around the indices** instead of inset from the card edge, after the human's 「你不觉得留白太多吗」.
- **The drawing's geometry** (pip places, sizes, frame, line width) lives as named constants in `playing-card.tsx`, in the face's own 500 × 700 units, like path data. These are the drawing, not design tokens, and the token check passes.
