# SKATGO-65 acceptance check plan

Free play on the built product, scripted legal moves until a deal is decided early (`tmp/reveal.mjs`).

## 1. A claim lays everyone's remaining cards open, with a line and the tap hand
- Met when: the learner's remaining cards are in the hand and both opponents' are face up, each
  under the owner's name; a line says who claimed what; the tap hand shows; no settlement yet.

## 2. Only a tap brings the settlement
- Met when: after one tap the settlement dialog appears and the reveal is gone.

## 3. A conceded Null is laid open the same way
- Met when: after the computers give up a Null, the same reveal shows before the settlement.
