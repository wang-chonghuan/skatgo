# SKATGO-151 grill

## Your decisions

- Two steps, contract then bid (「答题分两步吧，先点什么合约，再点最高叫到几点」); one step at a time with a verdict each,
  as proposed and not objected to.
- No set of ten (「不要10题，你就自然，先不用这个复杂」).
- Null is a contract to choose (「你还没说null呢」); the Null questions stay.
- The cheat sheet covers every point of the books' summary, clearer, not wordy (「你必须覆盖刚才书上原文所有的点……不能啰嗦和重复」).

## Recommendations you approved

- Step 2 asks the AI's contract's value whatever step 1 was; a pass question has no step 2.
- Fixed options in step 1; questions ordered easy to hard; the tile title one line and its height fixed.
- Answers given after opening the cheat sheet count as any other.

## Decided for you

1. **An attempt is recorded once complete** (after step 2, or step 1 when the AI passes). *Basis:* a half-answered
   question says nothing about the bid.
2. **Stats rows are exclusive**: passed a playable hand, played a hand to pass, wrong game, wrong value (right contract,
   wrong number). *Basis:* the human's 超叫 / 不敢叫 / 叫错 plus the counting step; shares add up to Wrong.
3. **Old answers** (`first`, today's) are read as the contract their number stood for; their value only when that contract
   was the AI's. *Basis:* nothing lost, nothing invented.
4. **Step 2 options**: the right multiplier and its neighbours (one less — forgetting "game" — and one more), never under
   2; Null 23/35/46/59.
5. **Explanation** from facts only (trumps, Aces, Jacks, with/without, the sum); no claim of why beyond them.
