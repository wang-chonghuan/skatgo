## Batch 1

### Q1. Which values for the four-colour scheme?

Your first table gives two for clubs and two for diamonds.

**Recommended:**
- **clubs `#00A859`** (the "高亮绿", bright green);
- **diamonds `#0071E3`** (the "电光蓝", electric blue).

**Why:** the pure `#008000` and `#0000FF` are harsh on white and read darker than the red. The
brighter pair sits closer to the German scheme's `#059669` and `#D97706` in weight, so switching
schemes does not change how heavy the cards look.

**Decision:** as recommended: clubs `#00A859`, diamonds `#0071E3` — human, 2026-09-30 (「只改颜色，不要改其他东西，避免扩大化，其他同意」).

### Q2. Where is the settings button?

**Recommended:**

  the band on the course and the lessons.
- **At the table:** as the side panel's second tab, "设置" (settings).
- **On a phone:** the gear stays in the band, beside the language menu.

It opens one small dialog (white, the table's corners) listing the three schemes: German Skat
(default), four-colour and two-colour. Each is shown as a row of ♣ ♠ ♥ ♦ in its own colours, with a
check on the current one. Tapping one applies it at once; there is no "save".

**Decision:** as recommended — human, 2026-09-30 (「只改颜色，不要改其他东西，避免扩大化，其他同意」).

### Q3. What follows the setting?

**Recommended:**
- **Follows:** every card face's pips and corner indices, and every suit symbol in text (lessons,
  drills, contract names, the front page's suits).
- **Stays as it is:** the court figures' own colours, the card back, and the contract picker's pale
  tile tints (they are backgrounds, not suit colours).

**Decision:** only the colours change. Suit colour on card pips and indices, and on suit symbols in text, follows the setting. Nothing else is restyled or moved: no other element, layout or component is touched to make room for this ("避免扩大化") — human, 2026-09-30 (「只改颜色，不要改其他东西，避免扩大化，其他同意」).
