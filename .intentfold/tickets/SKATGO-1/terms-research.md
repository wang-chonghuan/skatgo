## 1. Game name and the two face-down cards

| Concept | DE | EN | ZH | Basis (short) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Game name | Skat (das Skatspiel) | Skat | 斯卡特 | ISkO-DE 1.1.1 "Das Skatspiel"; ISkO-EN 1.1.1 "Skat"; ZH-WP title "斯卡特" |
| The 2 face-down cards | der Skat | the skat (lower-case in ISkO-EN; italic *Skat* in EN-WP) | **[底牌] — supported, but only indirectly.** ZH-WP calls them 斯卡特 / 斯卡特牌 ("两张斯卡特牌加入手牌"). ZH-Shengji uses 底牌 for the kitty ("最后的8张叫做底牌"). A Baidu/Zhihu search snippet reads "作为底牌（称为Skat）". | ISkO-DE 1.1.3 "Zwei verdeckt, gesondert gelegte Karten - Skat genannt"; ISkO-EN 1.1.3 "Two cards dealt face down, called the skat" |

**Recommendation: keep 底牌.** It is the normal Chinese card-game word for a kitty. You could gloss it once as "底牌（Skat）". ZH-WP's own term, 斯卡特牌, is ambiguous because 斯卡特 is also the name of the game.

Sources: ISkO-DE, ISkO-EN, ZH-WP, ZH-Shengji, EN-WP.

## 2. Declarer, defenders, partner

| Concept | DE | EN | ZH | Basis (short) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Declarer | **Alleinspieler** (colloquial: Solist, Spieler) | **declarer** (also: soloist) | **[庄家] — supported.** ZH-WP: "每一局会有一个庄家和两个闲家". | ISkO-DE definitions and 1.1.1 "von einem Alleinspieler und zwei Gegenspielern"; ISkO-EN definitions "Declarer: The player who wins the bidding"; Pagat "declarer"; EN-Gloss "Soloist or declarer" |
| One of the two opponents | **Gegenspieler** | **defender** (ISkO-EN, EN-WP); **opponent** (Pagat) | **[防守方] — partly supported.** ZH-WP uses **闲家**. ZH-Shengji lists all three: "庄家对手称为防家、闲家、防守方". | ISkO-DE "Gegenspieler: Die zwei Spieler, die gegen den Alleinspieler spielen"; ISkO-EN "Defender: Each of the two players who play against the declarer" |
| The defending side as a party | **Gegenpartei** | **opposition** (ISkO-EN); defending team (EN-WP) | 防守方 / 闲家 | ISkO-DE 3.4.1; ISkO-EN 3.4.1 |
| Partner (fellow defender) | **Partner** (DE-WP: "der Partner des Gegenspielers"; Skatinsel: "dem Partner Augen in den Stich legen") | **partner** (EN-Gloss: "if your partner already has the trick") | **[同伴] — supported** by related-game usage (ZH-Shengji and ZH-Bridge: "坐在同伴的对面"). ZH-WP Skat has no word for it. | DE-WP; EN-Gloss (Buttern); ZH-Shengji |

- **FLAG (ZH):** ZH-WP, the only Chinese Skat source, calls the defenders **闲家**, not 防守方. 防守方 is still correct Chinese (ZH-Shengji, ZH-Bridge). Keeping 防守方 is defensible, but it does not match ZH-WP.
- **FLAG (DE):** Do not use "Mitspieler" to mean partner. In the ISkO, *Mitspieler* means everyone at the table ("Mitspieler: Alle Personen am selben Tisch").

Sources: ISkO-DE, ISkO-EN, Pagat, EN-Gloss, DE-WP, ZH-WP, ZH-Shengji, ZH-Bridge, Skatinsel-DE.

## 3. Seats

| Concept | DE | EN | ZH | Basis (short) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forehand (left of dealer, receives cards first, leads to trick 1) | **Vorhand** | **forehand** (F) | **[前家] — CONTRADICTED.** ZH-WP uses **先手** ("其左手边的玩家为先手（Vorhand）"). | ISkO-DE 3.3.2 "Der Spieler, der zuerst Karten erhalten hat (Vorhand)"; ISkO-EN 3.3.2 "(forehand)"; Pagat |
| Middlehand | **Mittelhand** | **middlehand** (M) | **[中家] — CONTRADICTED.** ZH-WP uses **中间手**. | ISkO-DE 3.3.2; ISkO-EN 3.3.2 |
| Rearhand (the dealer, with 3 players) | **Hinterhand** | **rearhand** (R); EN-Gloss also lists "endhand" | **[后家] — CONTRADICTED.** ZH-WP uses **后手**. | ISkO-DE 3.3.3 "der dritte Spieler (Hinterhand)"; ISkO-EN 3.3.2; Pagat "If there are three players at the table R is the dealer" |
| Dealer | **Kartengeber** (ISkO); **Geber** (colloquial, DE-WP) | **dealer** | **发牌者** (ZH-WP: "发牌者（Geber）"). No [bracket] term given. | ISkO-DE 3.2.2; ISkO-EN 3.2.2 |

- **FLAG (ZH):** The one Chinese Skat source uses 先手 / 中间手 / 后手, not 前家 / 中家 / 后家. I found no Chinese Skat source that uses 前家/中家/后家. The Chinese Wikipedia terms have a drawback: 先手/后手 also mean "first move / second move" in board games, so they are ambiguous. 前家/中家/后家 is a coinage that no source supports but that is internally consistent. **This is a judgment call for the product owner.**

Sources: ISkO-DE, ISkO-EN, Pagat, EN-Gloss, ZH-WP.

## 4. Trick, lead, follow suit, trump (ruff), discard (throw off)

| Concept | DE | EN | ZH | Basis (short) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trick | **Stich** | **trick** | **[墩] — supported.** ZH-WP: "每一墩的赢家吃下这一墩（Stich）". | ISkO-DE 4.3 "Stiche"; ISkO-EN 4.3 "Tricks" |
| To lead | **ausspielen** (the first lead of a game: **anspielen**) | **to lead**; "opening lead" | **[首出] — weakly supported.** ZH-WP: "发牌者左侧的玩家先出牌" and calls the led card **引牌**. ZH-Shengji uses **领出** throughout, and 首出 only once, as a verb ("首出推土机时"). | ISkO-DE 4.1 "Ausspielen", 2.6.5 "vor dem ersten Ausspielen (Anspielen)"; ISkO-EN 4.1 "The Lead" |
| To follow suit | **bedienen** | **to follow suit** | **[跟牌] — supported.** ZH-Shengji uses 跟牌; ZH-WP: "必须跟出这种花色". | ISkO-DE 4.2 "Bedienen"; ISkO-EN 4.2 "Following Suit or Trump"; Pagat "Players must follow suit if they can" |
| To trump (ruff) | **stechen** / **einstechen** (overtrump: **überstechen**) | **to trump** ("ruff" is a bridge word; I saw it in none of the Skat sources) | **[毙] — supported.** ZH-Shengji: "将吃，也称为毙牌、杀牌". 将吃 is the more formal word. | ISkO-DE 4.2.2 "Trumpf zugeben, d.h. stechen"; DE-WP "einsticht … übersticht" |
| To discard / throw off (no suit to follow, not trumping) | **abwerfen** | **discard** / throw off | **[垫] — supported.** ZH-Shengji "垫出其他花色的牌，称为垫牌"; ZH-Bridge "墊牌". | DE-WP "unpassende Karten abzuwerfen"; Skatinsel-DE "Abwerfen – Nichttrumpf-Karte legen, wenn Bedienen unmöglich ist" |

- **Note (ZH):** 首出 is understandable. The standard Chinese words are 领出 (ZH-Shengji) or 引牌 / 首引 (ZH-WP and ZH-Bridge). **Unsure whether to change it**; no source contradicts 首出 outright.

Sources: ISkO-DE, ISkO-EN, Pagat, DE-WP, Skatinsel-DE, ZH-WP, ZH-Shengji, ZH-Bridge.

## 7. Ranks, written notation, nicknames, zero-point cards

| Concept | DE | EN | ZH | Basis (short) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ace / Ten / King / Queen / Jack / 9 / 8 / 7 | **Ass** (old: Daus), **Zehn**, **König**, **Dame** (Ober), **Bube** (Unter), **Neun**, **Acht**, **Sieben** | **Ace, Ten, King, Queen, Jack, Nine, Eight, Seven** | A, 10, K, Q, J, 9, 8, 7 (ZH-WP uses the letters; German index letters noted as "K（König）、D（Dame）… B（Bube）") | ISkO-DE 1.2.2 "Ass (Daus) 11 Augen … Bube (Unter) 2 Augen"; ISkO-EN 1.2.2 |
| One-letter abbreviations | **A, 10, K, D, B, 9, 8, 7**. DE-WP uses "7, 8, 9, 10, D, K, Ass" in running text; ZH-WP confirms the German index letters K/D/B. | **A, 10, K, Q, J, 9, 8, 7** (Pagat, EN-WP) | — | DE-WP; ZH-WP; Pagat |
| How cards are written in prose | Suit + hyphen + rank: **Kreuz-Bube, Pik-Bube, Herz-Ass, Pik-9, Herz-10, Kreuz-Dame**. The ISkO and German strategy literature write it this way, not "♣B". | "jack of clubs", "J♣" / "♣J" | ♣J etc. (ZH-WP uses the symbol plus letter, e.g. "♣J>♠J") | ISkO-DE 2.3.1 "vom Kreuz-Buben an"; ISkO-DE (Wissenswertes) "Kreuz-Dame, -9 und -8"; VG88 "Pik-9", "Herz-10", "Kreuz-Bube" |
| Nickname for ♣J | **der Alte** | "the Old Man" (only as a translation; English texts just say "jack of clubs") | no Chinese source | DE-WP image caption "den Kreuz-Buben, den „Alten“"; Skatinsel-DE "Der Alte – So wird liebevoll der Kreuz-Bube genannt"; EN-Gloss "*Der Alte* ('the Old Man'): Jack of Clubs" |
| 7/8/9 (0 points) | **Luschen** (singular Lusche); also Leere, Nullen | **blanks** | **[白板] — no Chinese source; keep current.** | DE-WP "Die übrigen (9, 8, 7), die auch Luschen genannt werden"; Skatinsel-DE "Luschen – Sammelbegriff für Karten, die 0 Augen bringen"; EN-WP "the blanks (*Luschen*)"; EN-Gloss "*Luschen*: blanks" |
| Aces and Tens together | **Volle** | "fulls" / "fat ones" | no Chinese source | Skatinsel-DE "Volle – … Asse und Zehner"; Skatinsel-EN "take your Fulls to the grave" |

- **On German notation:** I found no DSkV document that uses a compact "♣B" style. The official text spells cards out, as in "Kreuz-Bube". For compact UI labels, **♣B / ♠D / ♥K / ♦A / ♣10** follows the letters on German French-suited decks. That choice rests on DE-WP and ZH-WP, not on a DSkV rule. **Somewhat unsure** whether the Ace index is "A" on every German deck; DE-WP writes "Ass" in full in that passage.
- **FLAG (EN):** In Skat English, "blank" has two meanings. "blanks" means the 7/8/9 cards (EN-WP). "blank" also means a singleton or void suit (EN-Gloss: "*blank* (1): a singleton … *blank* (2): to be void"). The EN course should use "blanks" only for 7/8/9, and "singleton" or "void" for the other meaning.

Sources: ISkO-DE, ISkO-EN, DE-WP, EN-WP, EN-Gloss, Skatinsel-DE, Skatinsel-EN, VG88, ZH-WP, Pagat.

## 8. Card points and the 61 threshold

| Concept | DE | EN | ZH | Basis (short) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Card points | **Augen** (singular Auge) | **card points** (Pagat, EN-WP). ISkO-EN just says "points". | **[点/牌点] — CONTRADICTED in wording.** ZH-WP uses **牌分** ("分值（Augen，牌分）"). | ISkO-DE 1.2.2 "11 Augen … 120 Augen"; Pagat "the total number of card points in the pack is 120" |
| Declarer needs 61 | "Das 61. Auge gewinnt"; "mit dem 61. Auge" | "the declarer needs 61 card points" / "61 or more points" | "庄家获得至少61牌分" (ZH-WP) | ISkO-DE 2.5.4 "Einfach gewonnen hat der Alleinspieler sein Spiel mit dem 61. Auge einschließlich der beiden Karten im Skat"; ISkO-EN 2.5.4 "61 or more points, including points of the two skat cards" |
| Defenders need 60 | 60 Augen | 60 card points | 60 牌分 | DE-WP; Pagat |

- **FLAG (ZH):** ZH-WP says **牌分**, not 牌点. It also keeps 牌分 (card points) separate from **局分** (game value). Keeping those two apart matters in Skat. It is the same distinction as Augen vs. Spielwert, and Pagat stresses "card points … are quite separate from the game points". 牌点 is understandable but has no source.

Sources: ISkO-DE, ISkO-EN, Pagat, DE-WP, ZH-WP.

## 10. Game value: base value, multiplier, "game", matadors

| Concept | DE | EN | ZH | Basis (short) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base value | **Grundwert** (Karo 9, Herz 10, Pik 11, Kreuz 12, Grand 24) | **base value** (Pagat, EN-WP); ISkO-EN "basic value" | **[基值] — CONTRADICTED in wording.** ZH-WP: **基本分** ("基本分（Grundwert）"). | ISkO-DE 2.4.1 "Jedes Farb- und Grandspiel hat einen unveränderlichen Grundwert"; Pagat "the base value and the multiplier" |
| Multiplier | **Summe der Fälle** / "Fälle" (ISkO); colloquial "Spielstufe". Components: **Gewinnstufen** + Spitzen. | **multiplier** (Pagat); "game level" (EN-WP); ISkO-EN "sum of the hand" / "win levels" | **[倍数] — supported.** ZH-WP: "倍数（Fälle）为王牌分与达成分的和". | ISkO-DE 2.5.3 "Spitzen und Gewinnstufen werden addiert und ergeben die Summe der Fälle"; Pagat "The multiplier is the sum of all applicable items"; EN-WP "game level (*Spielstufe* or *Gewinngrad*)" |
| "Game" (+1, always counts) | **Spiel** ("Spiel einfach" = level 1) | **game** ("Game (always applies)") | **[成局] — CONTRADICTED.** ZH-WP names it **Spiel** and glosses it **底分** ("+1 Spiel 底分，每一局都有"). | ISkO-DE 2.5.1 "Spiel einfach"; Pagat multiplier table "Game (always applies) 1" |
| Win levels (Hand, Schneider, …) | **Gewinnstufen** | **win levels** (ISkO-EN); multipliers (Pagat) | 达成分 (ZH-WP) | ISkO-DE 2.5.1; ISkO-EN 2.5.1 |
| Matadors (unbroken top trumps from ♣J) | **Spitzen**; "**mit** 2" / "**ohne** 2" | **matadors** (Pagat, EN-WP); **"Tops"** in ISkO-EN; "**with** 2" / "**without** 2" (ISkO-EN, EN-WP) or "**against** 2" (Pagat) | **[有 2 / 无 2] — CONTRADICTED in wording.** ZH-WP: 王牌分（Spitze）, "**带**2（Mit 2）", "**缺**2（Ohne 2）". | ISkO-DE 2.3.1 "Trümpfe in ununterbrochener Reihenfolge vom Kreuz-Buben an heißen Spitzen", 2.3.2 "er spielt mit Spitzen … ohne Spitzen"; ISkO-EN 2.3.1 "called 'Tops'" |
| Counting aloud | "**Mit zwei, Spiel drei, mal Kreuz = 36**"; ISkO: "Mit 4, Spiel 5, Hand 6, Schneider 7, Schneider angesagt 8, Schwarz 9, Schwarz angesagt 10, offen 11 = 11 x 24 = 264" | "**with 2, game 3, Schneider 4, 4 times spades is 44**" (Pagat); "against 2, game 3, hand 4, 4 times clubs is 48" (Pagat) | ZH-WP has no spoken formula. It gives "局分＝基本分×倍数". | ISkO-DE (Wissenswertes, "Grand ouvert"); DE-WP "„mit zwei, Spiel: drei mal Kreuz“ = 3 · 12 = 36"; Pagat |
| Game value | **Spielwert** | **game value** | 局分 (ZH-WP: "局分（Spielwert）") | ISkO-DE 2.5.2; ISkO-EN 2.5.2 |

- **FLAG (ZH):** ZH-WP contradicts three of the course's wordings:
  - 基值 → ZH-WP uses **基本分**.
  - 成局 → ZH-WP uses **Spiel / 底分**.
  - 有2/无2 → ZH-WP uses **带2/缺2**.

  In Chinese card-game usage, 成局 usually means "a game/contract is made", so it may mislead readers.
- **EN choice:** "matadors" is the most common English word (Pagat, EN-WP, Parlett tradition). "Tops" is the official ISPA translation. For "with / without", ISkO-EN and EN-WP say "without" and Pagat says "against". Recommend **"with / without"** because it matches the official text and German *ohne*.

Sources: ISkO-DE, ISkO-EN, Pagat, EN-WP, DE-WP, ZH-WP.
