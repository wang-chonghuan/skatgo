# SKATGO-172 grill

No question passed the bar; decided:

1. A deal without the AI's result (a day dealt before the AI played it) counts as "same": 10 XP.
2. The week is Monday to Sunday of the tournament's days (Berlin time), as the daily boards'.
3. Streak: days in a row, up to today or yesterday, with at least one deal of any kind; otherwise "–".
4. The card lists every account with XP this week; the AI has no row (it earns no XP).
5. The deal's XP stays in the result dialog under the score, with "?" for the tiers; the toast (calculation in one line)
   shows once and goes after a few seconds or a tap.
6. A guest is told "Sign in to earn XP": a guest's free deal is not kept, so "keep" would not be true.
7. The Elo's narrow-list token is renamed for the XP card, its value unchanged (the card keeps the Elo card's size).
