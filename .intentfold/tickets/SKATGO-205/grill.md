# SKATGO-205 grill

Grill: human. No question passed the bar: the rules, numbers and behaviour are the ticket's (agreed in conversation
2026-11-17); the rest is cheap to change in a rework round or shown on screen.

## Decided for you

1. **A person away when the next deal comes.** Taken off the table like one who left: the seat goes to a persona, no
   fee; coming back means sitting down again. Basis: ticket (离开后不再给他发牌、不再扣费); otherwise an away player
   would be charged deal after deal while the AI plays for them.
2. **Nobody left online.** The table waits for the 30-second grace of whoever is away, then the sitting ends.
3. **"Leave the table" at the table** sends the leave and goes back to the League page; the current deal is finished by
   a computer for that seat and counts (ticket).
4. **No joining a running table.** A new person is matched at sitting down only (ticket: 不含中途加入).
5. **The deal's line**: "+3 · You won your game", "+3 · You beat the declarer", "−3 · You lost your game",
   "−1 · The declarer made it", "0 · All passed", and on a move " · Up to the Gold League!" / " · Down to the …".
6. **The reset** of existing League points runs once, on the first start of this release, keyed by a scale column so a
   restart never resets again.
7. **The bar** on the League card runs from −20 to +30.
8. **Live tables at the deploy** keep going under the new rules from their next deal.
