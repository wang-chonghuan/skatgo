Format: the upper half of a double-headed court card, landscape. The figure is shown from the top of its headwear down to the waist, centred, with its shoulders, sleeves and garments spreading across the whole width of the picture. Everything the figure holds stays inside the picture; nothing is cut off at the left, right or top edge. The figure is cut by a straight horizontal line exactly at the bottom edge of the picture, so the picture can be mirrored at that edge. Nothing below the cut: no legs, no ground, no shadow.
